About This Tracker

This independent project tracks games that reach Steam's Overwhelmingly Positive review classification.

Qualification criteria

  • Overwhelmingly Positive label
  • At least 500 total user reviews
  • At least 95% positive reviews

Milestone dates are taken from the tracker's recorded transition time. Games already qualifying on their first check have no known transition date. This project is not affiliated with Valve or Steam.
